For Keith: why this beats a Facebook roll call

  • 01
    One live count

    No reading through comments to tally who's coming. The count updates as riders sign up.

  • 02
    Automatic go or no-go

    When 10 riders are in, it's on and everyone is told. If not, everyone gets a "closed tonight" note without a separate post.

  • 03
    Reaches riders off Facebook

    Parents, new riders and anyone who missed the post still find out.

  • 04
    Answers "what time tomorrow?"

    Hours sit next to every ride day, so Google and AI assistants can answer it too, before anyone has to ask in the comments.

Questions riders ask

FAQ

Is Pineville MX Park open this weekend?

Check the number plate at the top of this page. Pineville opens on select weekends, and every open day is posted here and on Facebook.

How far is it from Charleston?

About an hour. The track is at 1812 Hwy 45 in Pineville, SC 29468, in Berkeley County.

How much does it cost to ride?

Practice is $20 per rider and $15 for 50cc riders. Spectators and parking are free. Race-day pricing is posted with each race.

Is there a track for kids?

Yes. There's a separate peewee track, and 50cc riders get a lower rate.

Is there midweek practice?

Sometimes. Wednesday practice, usually 3:00 to 8:00 PM, runs when at least 10 riders sign up through the ride call above.

Can I get riding coaching?

Yes. Riders work with Coach Keith at the track. Ask about times and rates when you call. TBC

Can I camp at the track?

Yes. Camping is primitive, with a vault toilet on site.

Can I bring a quad or a side-by-side?

Quads are welcome with a tether kill switch. UTVs and side-by-sides are not allowed.
